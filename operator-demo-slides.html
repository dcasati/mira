Slide 3 / Knowledge split

Two knowledge domains, one Operator

kb-zavatrix

Unstructured radio manuals and field notes: FT-818ND setup, procedures, troubleshooting, and equipment configuration.

ks-fabriciq-operator-matrix

Structured Fabric operational data: missions, events, assets, call signs, prowords, and ontology relationships.

Routing rule

Manual questions go to Foundry Knowledge. Operational questions go to Fabric IQ. Long instructions can be sent to Zello chat.

Slide 4 / Fabric IQ data model

Fabric operational model

Lakehouse tables

  • operator_events, simulation_assets
  • missions, mission_events
  • call_signs, operator_mission_manual
  • original_roles

Ontology

Mission links to Procedure, Asset, CallSign, and Event. Role links to CallSign and Mission. This gives Fabric IQ domain semantics.

Example: “What happened on the relay hardline?” → ASSET-004 → operator_events → two grounded events.
